Queries:
1. For Top products by revenue: 
	"select product_name, SUM(quantity) as 'total_quantity',SUM(quantity * price) as 'revenue'  from sales_data group by product_name order by revenue desc"

2. For Top 5 products by revenue:
	"select product_name, SUM(quantity) as 'total_quantity',SUM(quantity * price) as 'revenue'  from sales_data group by product_name order by revenue desc limit 5"

3. For top categories by revenue:
	"select category, SUM(quantity) as 'total_quantity',SUM(quantity * price) as 'revenue' from sales_data group by category order by revenue desc"

4. For Top Locations by revenue:
	"select location ,product_name, SUM(quantity) as 'total_quantity',SUM(quantity * price) as 'revenue'  from sales_data group by location order by revenue desc"
